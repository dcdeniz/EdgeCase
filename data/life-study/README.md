# LIFE Study controlled-source contract

The Longitudinal Investigation of Fertility and the Environment (LIFE) Study is
a prospective preconception cohort described by NICHD:

<https://www.nichd.nih.gov/about/org/diphr/od/research/longitudinal>

NICHD describes 501 couples recruited in Michigan and Texas, with two semen
samples from male participants, blood and urine specimens, interviews,
anthropometry, daily lifestyle journals, endocrine-disrupting chemical
measurements, time-to-pregnancy, and pregnancy outcomes.

## Access status

No participant-level LIFE data is stored in this repository. Treat it as
controlled access until the correct repository accession, approval process,
data-use agreement, and permitted fields are confirmed.

The dbGaP accession previously cited by the research prose,
`phs001692.v1.p1`, currently describes an inconsistent eight-subject
semen/microRNA repository. It must not be used as LIFE cohort data or metadata.

## Intended use in PreSeed

If access is approved, LIFE should remain a structured research dataset outside
the VectorRAG evidence index. It can support offline exposure/fecundity analysis
and validation. Peer-reviewed LIFE publications may separately become reviewed,
claim-sized RAG evidence.

Before any import, record:

- confirmed accession and dataset version;
- approval and data-use restrictions;
- participant and visit identifiers;
- specimen and collection timing;
- semen endpoint definitions and laboratory methods;
- exposure assay, unit, detection limit, and batch;
- lifestyle and journal measurement windows;
- couple-level time-to-pregnancy and pregnancy endpoints;
- missingness and censoring conventions; and
- export, retention, deletion, and publication restrictions.

Never commit controlled participant data to Git.
